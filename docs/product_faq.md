# Product FAQ 
Our app supports Android 10 and above, and iOS 15 and above.
Free plan users get 5 projects and 1 GB storage. Pro plan costs Rs 499 per month and gives unlimited projects and 50 GB storage.
You can cancel the Pro plan anytime from Settings > Billing. Refunds are available within 7 days of purchase.
Data is backed up automatically every 24 hours.
